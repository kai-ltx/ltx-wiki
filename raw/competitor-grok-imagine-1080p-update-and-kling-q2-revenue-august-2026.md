### Part 1 — xAI Grok Imagine: 2026-08-01 video upgrade

Distinct from the Grok Imagine Video 1.5 launch the wiki already records (June 3, 2026). This is an incremental August update, referred to as **Grok Imagine Video 1.5 / build v1.3.28**, rolled out **2026-08-01**:

- **Voice consistency** via a new **multi-channel audio reference** system — anchors generated content to specific voice profiles instead of drifting accents/timbre between scenes. Previous versions had lip-sync and audio generation but unstable cross-scene voice.
- **Native 1080p output** for both text-to-video and image-to-video pipelines — no post-processing upscaling step. Framed as a workflow win, not just quality.
- **Multi-reference support**: up to **7 image references** and **3 audio inputs** simultaneously, for character / environment / object consistency across generations.
- Built on xAI's Aurora-driven models.
- **All new capabilities are subscriber-only.** Free-tier X users have had image and video generation access "pulled back significantly" during 2026 — a deliberate monetization bet.
- xAI signals continued iterative cadence; community-driven video extension and prompt-based consistency controls said to be in the pipeline.
- **Pricing: $0.08 per second** for Grok Imagine Video 1.5 — reported as one of the cheapest frontier video models on the market. (Artificial Analysis lists grok-imagine-video-1.5 at $8.40/min for 1080p, and the older grok-imagine-video at $4.20/min.)

Adjacent Grok Imagine releases in window (image side, for context):
- **Grok Imagine Image 2.0** launched as the new **Quality Mode** on grok.com/imagine and the Grok iOS/Android apps: stronger instruction following, sharper text, precise region-level editing, smart resize, templates, multi-image compositing, multi-reference generation. As of **2026-08-07** it sat **#2 on Arena leaderboards for both text-to-image and image editing**, behind OpenAI's gpt-image-2.
- **Native meme generation** announced by Elon Musk **2026-08-09**.

Arena standing (AA, 2026-08-24): grok-imagine-video-1.5 is **#4 in image-to-video with audio at Elo 1,111**; grok-imagine-video is #11 at 1,076 and #21 in T2V-with-audio at 1,062.

### Part 2 — Kling AI Q2 2026 financials (reported 2026-08-19/20)

Kuaishou released Q2 2026 results on **2026-08-19**; TechNode/Caixin coverage 2026-08-20.

- **Kling AI Q2 revenue: more than RMB 850 million** (~US$117-120M), **up more than 200% year on year** and **up 30% quarter on quarter**.
- **Kling AI H1 2026 revenue: RMB 1.5 billion.**
- Kuaishou group Q2 total revenue **RMB 35.5 billion** (~$5.3B), up only **1.4% YoY**.
- Kuaishou **adjusted net profit fell 30% to RMB 3.9 billion** (other coverage reports net profit down 36%).
- Advertising and livestreaming revenue both under pressure — **Kling AI is now one of Kuaishou's clearest growth businesses**, making it structurally more important to the parent.
- Company aims to keep free cash flow positive in H2 2026.
- Context (already in wiki, not re-reported): July 2026 independent financing round of >RMB 19bn / ~$2.8B at ~$18B valuation with Tencent, Alibaba and Baidu participating, reducing Kuaishou's stake to ~68%.

**Note on Kling model releases:** searches surfaced "Kling 3.0 Turbo" and "Kling 3.0 Omni / native 4K" as August items, but both actually launched **2026-06-17** — out of window. No new Kling *model* release was found between 2026-07-21 and 2026-08-24; the in-window Kling news is financial. - **Regulatory (adjacent, may belong to another beat):** **EU AI Act Article 50 transparency obligations took effect 2026-08-02.** European Commission adopted guidelines **2026-07-20**. Providers of AI systems generating or manipulating synthetic audio/image/video/text must **embed machine-readable markings and provide a detection mechanism**. Fines up to **EUR 15 million or 3% of worldwide annual turnover**, whichever is higher. Applies globally to anyone placing AI on the EU market or whose outputs are used in the EU. Obligations apply immediately to all in-scope systems regardless of when placed on market; **content published before 2026-08-02 need not be retroactively labeled**; a transitional period gives providers of generative AI systems already on market until **2026-12-02** for the marking/detection obligation. The AI Office published a voluntary **Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-Generated Content** (including a standard icon set); several major AI providers have signed, gaining presumption of conformity and a more favorable enforcement posture, while non-signatories face closer scrutiny.
