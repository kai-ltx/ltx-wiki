# Runway Agent Launch: Conversational AI Video Creation (May 2026)

**Source:** https://runwayml.com/news/introducing-runway-agent
**Date:** 2026-05-13
**Retrieved:** 2026-05-19

## Content

Runway launched Runway Agent on May 13, 2026 — an agentic creative partner that takes a user from an idea to a fully finished, sound-designed and edited video through a single conversation.

### How It Works
The agent proposes a concept, develops story beats, lays out a full visual direction, and then builds the video with multiple scenes, voiceover, dialogue, and music — all assembled and ready to publish. The workflow covers concepting, story structure, visual direction, voiceover, dialogue, music, and final assembly in one process.

### Target Audience
Built for brand teams, marketers, creative agencies, and filmmakers who need high volumes and variety of video output: product launches, brand campaigns, social content, short films.

### Design Philosophy
Runway Agent is positioned as an AI that understands creative goals, builds toward them, and hands control back to the user at every step. It pulls from brand context, works across models, and produces at a scale and speed that Runway says "changes what's possible."

### April–May 2026 API Changelog Context
Alongside the Agent launch, Runway's API received several additions:
- **April 7, 2026:** Seedance 2.0 available for Unlimited and Enterprise plan users (outside the US) — text, image, video, and audio inputs; image-to-video, text-to-video, and video-to-video modes; 4–15 second durations.
- **April 17, 2026:** Seedance 2.0 added to the Runway API.
- **April 23, 2026:** OpenAI GPT Image 2 added to Runway API — up to 16 reference images, 30 aspect ratios, 1K/2K/4K tiers.
- **April 30, 2026:** Gemini 3 Pro Image ("Nano Banana Pro") added to Runway API — up to 14 reference images, 5,500-character prompts, 1K/2K/4K resolutions across 10 aspect ratios.

### Gen-4.5 Status
Gen-4.5 (released December 2025) remains Runway's flagship video model. With an Elo score of 1,247 on the Artificial Analysis Text-to-Video benchmark, it currently holds the top leaderboard position ahead of Google Veo 3.1, Kling 3.0, and Sora 2. The model supports text-to-video and image-to-video modes with 2–10 second clip durations.
