# Runway Gen-4.5: Benchmark Dominance and 60-Second Generation

**Source:** https://runwayml.com/research/introducing-runway-gen-4.5
**Date:** 2026-01-21
**Retrieved:** 2026-06-01

## Content

Runway Gen-4.5 is the current flagship video generation model from Runway. Released in January 2026, it holds the top position on the Artificial Analysis Text-to-Video leaderboard as of June 2026.

### Benchmark Performance
- **Elo score: 1,247** on Artificial Analysis Text-to-Video benchmark (June 2026)
- Leads over Google Veo 3 (1,226 Elo) and OpenAI Sora 2 Pro (1,206 Elo)
- Evaluated on motion quality and prompt adherence in blind A/B comparisons

### Key Capabilities
- **60-second continuous generation** — longest single-generation duration available as of June 2026
- Most granular camera control options in the market
- HD text-to-video and image-to-video with advanced physics modeling
- Developed entirely on NVIDIA GPUs

### Known Limitations
- Causal reasoning: effects can precede causes in generated clips
- Object permanence issues: objects may appear/disappear unexpectedly
- No real-time generation capability

### Pricing (June 2026)
- Available from $12/month on paid Runway plans
- API access: Gen-4 Image endpoint available (separate from video)

### Context vs LTX
- Gen-4.5 is a closed, proprietary API product vs LTX-2.3 which is open weights
- LTX-2.3 Elo: 1,121 on same benchmark (March 2026 snapshot) — gap of ~126 Elo points
- LTX-2.3 advantage: runs locally, no per-generation fees, open weights for fine-tuning
