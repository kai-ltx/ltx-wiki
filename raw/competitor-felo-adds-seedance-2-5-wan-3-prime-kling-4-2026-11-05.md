# Felo AI Video Adds Seedance 2.5, Wan 3.0 Prime and Kling 4.0

**Source:** https://felo.ai/blog/felo-video-new-models-seedance-wan-kling/
**Date:** 2026-11-05
**Retrieved:** 2026-11-09

## Content

Felo (aggregator, consumer AI search app) added three video models to its AI Video Generator on 2026-11-05, joining MiniMax H3. Capabilities are as exposed by Felo, checked Nov 5, and not each provider's maximum.

| Model | Duration | Resolution (Felo) | Inputs | Audio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Seedance 2.5 | 4-30 s | 480P / 720P | image, video, audio refs, or first+last frame | generated with video |
| Wan 3.0 Prime | 2-30 s | up to 1080P | text, first+last frame (no reference video, no 21:9) | optional generated audio |
| Kling 4.0 | 3-30 s | 720P / 1080P | reference images + videos; first, middle and last frame | **no soundtrack via Felo** |
| MiniMax H3 (existing) | 4-30 s at 480P/768P; 4-15 s at 1080P/2K | up to 2K | image, video, audio refs | listed as silent |

- "Wan 3.0 Prime" is a tier name not seen on Alibaba Cloud announcements so far; treat as Felo's label for a Wan 3.0 variant.
- Gemini Omni Flash marked "coming soon" on Felo.
- Context from Magic Hour tracker (same retrieval): Kling's own Oct 26 news post confirms Kling 4.0 launched Oct 15 with native 3-30 s generation, up to 15 combined reference assets and up to 10 keyframe inputs. Felo exposes only 3 keyframes.
- Relevance: 30-second single-pass clips are now the norm across Seedance 2.5, Wan 3.0 and Kling 4.0 on third-party hosts. LTX-2.5 API tops out at 20 s per clip per Replicate listing.
