# OpenAI Sora Shutdown — App Discontinued April 26, 2026

**Source:** https://www.indiewire.com/news/breaking-news/openai-shut-down-sora-generative-ai-video-app-1235185950/
**Secondary Source:** https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12593142-sora-release-notes
**Date:** 2026-04-26
**Retrieved:** 2026-05-25

## Content

OpenAI discontinued the Sora product on **April 26, 2026**. The Sora app is no longer available. The Sora 2 video generation API is deprecated and scheduled to shut down on **September 24, 2026**.

### Timeline

- **December 2024:** Sora originally launched publicly
- **September 30, 2025:** Sora 2 released with iOS app; Android app followed ~2 months later
- **April 26, 2026:** Sora app shut down; Disney partnership reportedly also ended
- **September 24, 2026:** Sora 2 Videos API planned end-of-life

### Sora 2 Capabilities (Before Shutdown)

Sora 2 was described as a major leap over the original:
- Olympic gymnastics, backflips on a paddleboard modeling buoyancy/rigidity physics
- Triple axels and advanced physical simulation
- Multi-shot instruction following with world state persistence
- Controllability across complex prompts

### Context and Competitive Implications

The Sora shutdown is significant for the competitive landscape:
- OpenAI is apparently pivoting away from standalone video generation as a product
- The Sora API will remain for developers until Sept 24, 2026
- Competing models (Runway Gen-4.5, Kling 3.5, LTX-2.3, Wan 2.7) continue active development
- Runway Gen-4.5 now holds #1 position on Artificial Analysis Video benchmark (1,247 Elo)
- The "Sora vs. Runway" and "Sora vs. LTX" comparison content that proliferated in 2025 is now outdated
