# Kling AI Raises $2.8 Billion at $18 Billion Valuation — July 2026

**Source:** https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/03/kuaishou-shares-fall-after-securing-tencent-funding-for-kling-ai.html, https://techfundingnews.com/kling-ai-closes-3b-round-at-18b-valuation-as-alibaba-and-tencent-back-chinas-sora-rival/
**Date:** 2026-07-03
**Retrieved:** 2026-07-06

## Content

Kling AI (Kuaishou spinoff) closed a $2.8 billion funding round on July 3, 2026 at an **$18 billion post-money valuation** ($15B pre-money). This is one of the largest single funding rounds in AI video generation history.

### Investors
- **Tencent** ($200M)
- **Alibaba Cloud**
- **Baidu**
- CITIC Securities
- Industrial and Commercial Bank of China
- 38 investors total

### Ownership
Kuaishou's stake in Beijing Kling diluted from 100% to approximately **68.33%** after the round closes.

### Revenue Trajectory
- March 2025: $100M ARR
- January 2026: $300M ARR
- March 2026: $500M ARR (a 5x increase in 12 months)
- Q1 2026 revenue: 650M yuan (~$96M), up 300%+ year-over-year

### IPO Plans
Kling is targeting a **Hong Kong Stock Exchange listing within 12 months** of final payment date.

### Strategic Context
The backing of Tencent, Alibaba, and Baidu simultaneously signals a Chinese tech sector consensus that AI video generation is a critical infrastructure play. The $18B valuation positions Kling as the highest-valued pure-play AI video generation company globally — exceeding Runway's last known valuation.

EU AI Act Article 50 enforcement (August 2, 2026) adds a data-sovereignty angle: Kling's China-based data processing creates compliance complexity for European enterprise customers.
