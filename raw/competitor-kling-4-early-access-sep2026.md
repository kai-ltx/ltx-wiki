# Kling 4.0 Enters Early Access (Sep 28, 2026); Full Launch Set for October

**Source:** https://kling.ai/blog/kling-4-ai-video-model
**Date:** 2026-09-30 (article), early access rollout began 2026-09-28
**Retrieved:** 2026-10-05

## Content

Kuaishou's Kling AI began rolling out early access to "Kling 4.0" and a faster "Kling 4.0 Flash" variant on **Sep 28, 2026**, to a limited group of users (Kling Ultra subscribers). The full Kling 4.0 model is **scheduled to launch in October 2026** (not yet fully released as of this retrieval — this is a tease/early-access window, following on from the Sep 27 "Kling 4.0" X teaser logged in a prior wiki update).

**Key specs/features (per official blog, Sep 30, 2026):**
- **Duration:** native 3-30 second single-pass generation (vs Kling 3.0's shorter ceiling).
- **Keyframes:** up to 10 flexible keyframes along the timeline (vs fewer on 3.0).
- **Omni Reference:** up to **15 combined reference assets** per generation — up to 10 images (subject refs, turnarounds, grids, storyboards, wireframes), up to 5 videos (combined ≤30s, including white-model/depth-map video), and up to 7 subject references (incl. up to 3 video-based subjects + voice references for vocal identity).
- **Resolution/format:** 4K output, 21:9 ultrawide support; **10-bit HDR at 1080p/4K is "coming soon"** (not yet shipped).
- **Audio:** higher-quality two-channel stereo, improved lip-sync accuracy, better ambient "sense of space."
- **Motion:** improved stability for large-scale/fast motion and complex camera work (orbits, tracking shots) — cited fight-scene example maintaining posture/limb relationships through rapid action.
- **Editing:** multimodal video editing — up to 5 video inputs per edit task (one primary + up to 30s combined), can add/replace/remove elements, change backgrounds, adjust expressions/colors/materials/camera angles while preserving kept parts of the scene.
- **Prompting:** supports prompts up to 8,000 tokens; improved multilingual on-screen text/logo/emoji rendering (10+ languages) that stays legible through camera movement and transitions.
- **New UI:** redesigned creation page — unified multimodal input box, Grid/List views, timeline-based editing, plus "Canvas" (visual asset organization) and "Agent" (conversational creative assistance).

**Kling 4.0 Flash:** lighter/faster sibling, up to 20s in a single pass at up to 720p, in limited early access since Sep 28 alongside the full model.

## Assessment

This is the most significant competitor model news of the window. If the October full launch specs match the early-access blog (30s native generation, 15-asset Omni Reference, 4K/21:9, improving on Kling 3.0's already-strong position), Kling 4.0 would extend Kuaishou's lead in duration and reference-based consistency — both areas where LTX-2.5 currently lags (LTX-2.5 tops out well short of 30s native, and lacks an equivalent multi-asset reference system).
