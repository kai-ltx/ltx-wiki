# LTX Explore — New No-Code Self-Service Product Launch

**Source:** https://ltx.io/release-notes ; https://app.ltx.io/
**Date:** 2026-07-20
**Retrieved:** 2026-07-21

## Content

Lightricks launched **LTX Explore**, positioned as "All the Power of LTX. None of the Setup." — a new self-service, no-code product tier sitting between the full LTX Studio production platform and the raw developer API.

**Key points:**
- No API integration, no technical setup, no engineering required — direct browser interface at app.ltx.io
- "30+ Production-Ready Capabilities": generation, post-production, audio processing, creative controls, styles, effects, camera motion, and more, all exposed through simple prompts
- Targeted at non-technical team members (creatives, designers, marketers, producers) rather than just engineers — "one prompt, one click"
- **Community-driven expansion**: new LoRAs and IC-LoRAs are added weekly by the LTX team and community creators; each new asset becomes a new capability automatically available to existing users with no action required
- Positioned as a distinct access tier alongside LTX Studio (full production suite) and the LTX API (for engineering integration)

This continues Lightricks' pattern (also seen with LTX Studio Canvas and Flows) of adding lighter-weight, less setup-intensive surfaces on top of the core LTX-2.3 model, likely aimed at widening the top of the funnel for creators who don't want a full production-suite subscription or API integration.
