# Luma AI Ray3.14: Eliminating Quality-Speed-Cost Tradeoff (January 2026)

**Source:** https://lumalabs.ai/press/luma-ai-launches-ray3.14
**Date:** 2026-01-26
**Retrieved:** 2026-05-19

## Content

Luma AI launched Ray3.14, an update to its Ray3 reasoning video model, on January 26, 2026. The release was announced across multiple outlets including BusinessWire and Yahoo Finance.

### Core Performance Claims
- **4x faster** generation speed compared to predecessor Ray3.
- **3x lower cost** per second of generated video.
- **Native 1080p** output (no upscaling required).
- Designed specifically to eliminate the quality-speed-cost tradeoff that previously required users to choose between faster/cheaper or better output.

### Key New Features

**Character Seeds**
Allows users to maintain identity consistency across multiple generated clips — a named "seed" for a character that can be reused across separate generations.

**Modify with Instructions**
Natural language editing of already-generated content, enabling directed changes across VFX, advertising, film, and design workflows without full regeneration.

**Improved Motion Control**
More precise motion control and stronger temporal consistency compared to earlier Ray3 versions.

### Availability
Ray3.14 is available in Dream Machine, Luma's consumer-facing product. Enterprise API access also available.

### Pricing Tiers (as of 2026)
- **Free:** 30 daily credits
- **Lite:** $9.99/month — 1,600 monthly credits
- **Plus:** $29.99/month — 4,200 monthly credits + 1080p upscale
- **Unlimited:** $94.99/month — unlimited Relaxed-speed generations

### Context: Ray3.14 Position in 2026 Market
Ray3.14 is Luma's active flagship model as of May 2026. The model competes directly with Runway Gen-4.5, Kling 3.0, and Wan 2.7. Luma's broader product suite includes Genie 3D (for 3D asset generation) and the Dream Machine consumer platform.
