# Pika 2.5: Next-Gen AI Video Engine with Scene Extension and Audio Sync (January 2026)

**Source:** https://pikartai.com/pika-2-5/
**Date:** 2026-01-26
**Retrieved:** 2026-05-19

## Content

Pika Labs announced Pika 2.5 on January 26, 2026, positioning it as its flagship video generation model and a major step up from Pika 2.2.

### Output Specifications
- **Resolution:** 1080p
- **Max duration:** Up to 25 seconds (via iterative extension with Pikaframes)
- **Native clip length:** Up to ~15 seconds; scene extension pushes to 25 seconds by using final frames as conditioning context for the next pass.

### Key New Features

**Layered Motion Control (Camera as First-Class Citizen)**
Pika 2.5 is described as the first Pika release that treats camera language as a first-class citizen. Users can influence camera direction, subject action, and environmental motion at the prompt level, with separate layered controls.

**Scene Extension / Pikaframes**
Scene extension lets generations build past the original clip boundary by treating final frames as conditioning context for the next pass — enabling iterative extension up to 25 seconds total.

**Improved Material Realism**
Materials render more accurately: leather grain, brushed aluminum, fabric weave, skin pores — less "AI smoothness," more production-ready footage according to Pika's release notes.

**Integrated Sound Effects**
Pika 2.5 includes automatic, context-aware sound effect generation synced to on-screen action (e.g., a car crash generates the corresponding metal crunch sound).

**Pikaformance**
A new performance engine that brings still images to life with hyper-real facial expressions synced to audio — enables talking/singing avatars from photos, characters, or mascots.

### Market Position (as of May 2026)
Pika 2.5 is the active mainstream Pika model. It competes against Kling 3.0, Runway Gen-4.5, and Luma Ray3.14 in the mid-to-high quality AI video generation segment. Prior model Pika 2.2 had been released February 27, 2025.
