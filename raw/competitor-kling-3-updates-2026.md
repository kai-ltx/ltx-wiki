## Content

### Kling 3.0 (February 5, 2026)

Kuaishou released Kling 3.0 on February 5, 2026 as a major architecture shift.

**Architecture: Multi-modal Visual Language (MVL)**
- Text, images, audio, and video all processed in one unified system
- Native 4K output
- Built-in multilingual audio support
- Creative tooling competing with Seedance 2.0, Sora 2 (now discontinued), and Veo 3.1

### Kling 3.5 (Mid 2026)

- Supports **native 1080p resolution at 60 frames per second**
- Cinematic frame rate without the "soap opera" look
- Key for professional editor workflows

### May 2026 Browser-Based Platform

In May 2026, Kling transitioned to a fully **browser-based experience**:
- Complex scenes render without high-end local hardware
- Reduces barrier to entry significantly
- Strategic shift to compete more directly with LTX Studio (cloud-based) vs. LTX Desktop (local)

### Kling 2.6

Available on Envato VideoGen — specifically noted for an "audio update" in Kling 2.6.

### Competitive Position vs. LTX-2.3

| Feature | Kling 3.x | LTX-2.3 |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| Model type | Closed/cloud | Open-source, runs locally |
| Native audio | Yes (MVL) | Yes |
| Resolution | 4K native | Up to 4K via upscaler |
| Frame rate | 60fps (3.5) | 48fps native |
| Local inference | No | Yes (LTX Desktop) |
| LoRA ecosystem | Limited | Growing (IC-LoRA, Cameraman, ID) |

Kling's browser transition mirrors LTX Studio's cloud-first strategy, while LTX maintains a differentiated open/local track via LTX Desktop.
