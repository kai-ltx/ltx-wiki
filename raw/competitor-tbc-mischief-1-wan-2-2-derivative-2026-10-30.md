# TBC Mischief 1: Neuron-Derived Image-to-Video Model Built on Wan 2.2

**Source:** https://thenextweb.com/news/tbc-mischief-neuron-derived-ai-video-model
**Date:** 2026-10-30
**Retrieved:** 2026-11-02

## Content

- The Biological Computing Co. (San Francisco) released TBC Mischief 1, an image-to-video model built on Alibaba's open Wan 2.2, on Oct 30, 2026 (PR Newswire release the same day).
- Available on fal and OpenRouter. Unite.AI reports free access through Nov 2.
- Claims: about 5x faster than Wan 2.2 at the same quality (Unite.AI adds "80% lower cost"). TNW: no independent benchmarks published.
- Price: TNW says USD 0.005 per second (5 s clip = 2.5 cents); Unite.AI says USD 0.02 per second. Conflicting; check fal pricing page.
- Method: the company studies lab-grown neuron networks and turns findings into a small software add-on that runs on standard AI chips. No prompts reach living cells. Unite.AI notes the underlying neuron research used Oasis (an interactive Minecraft video model), not Wan 2.2.
- Follows a September 2026 AWS deal to sell TBC models to businesses.
- Relevance to LTX: another example of open-weights derivatives (here Wan 2.2) being commercialised on fal at very low per-second prices, below LTX-2.5 Fast's USD 0.09/s at 720p on fal.
