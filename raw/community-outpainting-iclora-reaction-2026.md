**Source:** https://x.com/AIWarper/status/2042363422533435687 ; https://medium.com/@dremfind/ltx-reframe-solved-the-biggest-headache-in-multi-platform-video-e03ec141d2c5

## Content

Community reaction to LTX's outpainting capability (both the official Reframe API shipped 2026-07-13 and a community-trained outpainting IC-LoRA for LTX-2.3) has been positive:

- X/Twitter user **A.I.Warper** tested "the new outpainting IC-LoRA for LTX 2.3" and called it "pretty impressive," sharing a link to the LoRA + ComfyUI workflow.
- A Medium writeup ("LTX Reframe Solved the Biggest Headache in Multi-Platform Video") frames the Reframe API as solving a long-standing pain point for creators who need to repurpose a single hero video across multiple platform aspect ratios (16:9 YouTube, 9:16 TikTok/Reels/Shorts, 1:1 Instagram feed) without reshooting or manually painting in new content.
- ComfyUI tutorial coverage (YouTube: "ComfyUI Tutorial Extend Your Videos with LTX 2.3 Outpainting") and hosted workflow listings (MimicPC) followed within days of the feature's release, indicating fast community adoption/tutorial-ization — consistent with the pattern seen after prior LTX-2.3 IC-LoRA releases (Union Control, Motion Track, 3DREAL).

## Broader stack narrative

Comparison content published around this period (e.g. nemovideo.com "LTX 2.3 vs WAN 2.2", apatero.com "LTX-2 vs Wan vs Kling") continues to reinforce a "no single winner" consensus:
- **LTX-2.3**: fastest iteration speed (a 5s 720p clip that takes ~4-5 min on Wan 2.2 completes in under a minute on LTX-2.3), native 9:16, one-pass audio, and the only major open model with a documented licensed-training-data + clean Apache 2.0 commercial story.
- **Wan 2.2/2.5**: still preferred for cinematic camera work and narrative content where motion quality matters most.
- **Kling**: preferred for dynamic/expressive movement and native audio-driven dialogue/singing generation in one pass.

The recurring community framing is that professional creators now run a "stack" of models chosen per use case rather than standardizing on one.
