# Kling 3.0 Official Launch: Video 3.0, Video 3.0 Omni, Image 3.0 Omni

**Source:** https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/06/12/tmt-newswire/plentisoft/kling-ai-launches-30-model-ushering-in-an-era-where-everyone-can-be-a-director/2364248
**Date:** 2026-06-12
**Retrieved:** 2026-06-15

## Content

On June 12, 2026, Kling AI (Kuaishou) officially announced the public launch of its Kling 3.0 model series: **Video 3.0**, **Video 3.0 Omni**, **Image 3.0**, and **Image 3.0 Omni**. The announcement was distributed from Beijing via PR Newswire.

The 3.0 series is built on the Multi-modal Visual Language (MVL) framework and a unified training framework that handles text, image, audio, and video input/output in a single architecture.

### Video 3.0 — Key Features

- **Native audio across languages & accents:** English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish; American/British/Indian accents; multi-character dialogue in different languages
- **Extended duration:** Up to 15 seconds (up from 10s in prior generation)
- **Multi-shot storytelling:** Understands multi-scene, multi-shot instructions; adjusts camera angles dynamically; supports shot-reverse-shot, cross-cutting, voiceover
- **Text preservation in imagery:** Can maintain readable logos, signage, and branded text throughout video — useful for e-commerce
- **Photorealistic characters** with expressive, dynamic performances
- **Improved element consistency** via reference video + multiple image references

### Video 3.0 Omni — Key Features

- Reference video extraction: extracts visual traits and voice characteristics from a reference video, then replicates them across new scenes
- **Multi-shot storyboard feature:** specify duration, shot size, perspective, narrative content, and camera movements per shot
- Builds on "Elements" feature from Kling Video O1

### Image 3.0 Omni — Key Features

- 2K and 4K ultra-high-definition image output
- Exceptional texture, lighting, and material quality preservation
- Designed for professional production assets and virtual scene visualization

### Availability

- Kling 3.0 models available immediately for **Ultra subscribers** (early access)
- General public rollout announced as "coming soon"

### Company Metrics (as of June 2026)

- 60 million creators worldwide (up from earlier reported figures)
- 600 million+ videos produced
- 30,000+ enterprise clients
- Operates in 224 countries/regions

### Context

Kling 3.0 was first announced at launch in February 2026, but the detailed multi-model lineup (including Omni variants) was revealed officially on June 12. The [[competitor-kling]] wiki page previously tracked Kling 3.5 (May 2026) as the most recent model; this June 12 announcement clarifies that the 3.0 multi-variant lineup with Omni features is now in formal public launch phase.
