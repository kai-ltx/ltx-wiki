**Date:** 2026-06-12

## Content

Kling AI (Kuaishou Technology) officially launched its Kling 3.0 model series on June 12, 2026, including four models: **Video 3.0**, **Video 3.0 Omni**, **Image 3.0**, and **Image 3.0 Omni**. The launch is positioned as a shift "from basic video generation to sophisticated professional orchestration" using the **Multi-modal Visual Language (MVL) framework**.

### Video 3.0: Key Features

- **Native Audio Across Languages & Accents**: Supports English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish plus accents (American, British, Indian). Can produce multi-character scenes where each character speaks a different language.
- **Extended Video Duration**: Up to 15 seconds per clip (previous Kling 2.6 was up to 10s).
- **Intelligent multi-shot storytelling**: Understands multi-scene, multi-shot instructions. Dynamically adjusts camera angles — from shot-reverse-shot dialogue to cross-cutting and voice-over. Up to 6 connected shots per request.
- **Text preservation in imagery**: Can retain/generate text (signage, captions, branded elements) with high accuracy — valuable for e-commerce advertising.
- **Photorealistic output**: Lifelike characters with expressive, dynamic performances.

### Video 3.0 Omni: Advanced Storyboarding and Reference Control

- Builds on the "Elements" feature from Kling Video O1.
- Upload a reference video to extract visual traits and voice characteristics and replicate them across new scenes.
- **Multi-shot storyboard feature**: Specify duration, shot size, perspective, narrative content, and camera movements for each shot in storyboarding.

### Image 3.0 Omni: Ultra-High-Resolution Visuals

- Supports **2K and 4K** ultra-high-definition output.
- Exceptional texture, lighting, and material quality with high precision and consistency.

### Architecture

- Built on the **Multi-modal Visual Language (MVL) framework**, integrating text, images, audio, and video in a unified training framework.
- Integrates multiple tasks (T2V, I2V, reference-to-video, in-video editing) into a single native multimodal architecture.
- Follows complex narrative logic, delivers precise shot control, and maintains strong prompt adherence.

### Availability

- Available as **exclusive early access to Ultra subscribers** at launch; public rollout pending.
- Access URL: https://kling.ai/

### Scale

- 60 million creators worldwide (as of June 2026)
- 600 million+ videos produced
- 30,000+ enterprise clients
- Service in 224 countries and regions
