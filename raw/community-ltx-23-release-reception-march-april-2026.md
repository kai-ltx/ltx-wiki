## Content

### Release Summary
Lightricks released LTX-2.3 on March 5, 2026, marketed as the first open-weights model to credibly remove quality compromises across multiple dimensions simultaneously. It is a 22B parameter open-source audio–video model.

### Community Reception on Reddit (r/StableDiffusion)
The launch announcement thread on r/StableDiffusion accumulated over 700 upvotes and 147 comments, marking it as one of the more popular open-source video model releases in that community. Users highlighted:
- Rebuilt VAE with sharper detail retention
- Native portrait mode (9:16) output — especially welcomed for social content creators
- Improved image-to-video quality over LTX-2.0
- Integrated one-pass audio/vocoder generation
- Updated official ComfyUI nodes shipped alongside the release

Community user Aurel Manea commented: "results were indeed much better than LTX 2.0 regarding movement and coherence."

### Download Momentum and Ecosystem Growth
Since the January 2026 full open-source release of LTX-2, nearly 5 million downloads were recorded. Community-driven developments followed rapidly:
- EasyCache optimization achieved 2.3x inference speedup
- Quantized models appeared for consumer hardware configurations Lightricks had never officially tested
- Community-built LoRAs for styles and motion control proliferated on Hugging Face
- Third-party ComfyUI nodes extended the model beyond official capabilities

### Benchmark Position
LTX-2.3 Fast ranked #1 among all open-weight video models on the Artificial Analysis leaderboard at Elo 1121, placing it 10 Elo points ahead of Wan 2.2 at time of release.

### Sentiment Summary
Overall community sentiment: strongly positive, with enthusiasm focused on speed, native portrait support, open-source accessibility, and rapid iteration cadence from Lightricks.
