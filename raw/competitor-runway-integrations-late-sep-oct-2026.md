# Runway: Late-September/Early-October 2026 Integration Updates (No New Flagship Model)

**Source:** https://runway.com/changelog
**Date:** 2026-09-29 to 2026-10-02
**Retrieved:** 2026-10-05

## Content

Runway's changelog shows continued rapid tooling/integration cadence but **no new frontier video model** in-window — Gen-4.5 (Dec 2025) remains Runway's last flagship release, consistent with prior wiki entries.

In-window items:

- **Oct 2, 2026 — Seedance 2.5 Draft Mode in Agent (All Plans):** Agent can now generate with Seedance 2.5's Draft mode, producing fast 480p drafts with post-generation enhancement. Draft mode is auto-suggested and toggleable, meant to cut iteration cost/time before committing to a full-res render.
- **Oct 2, 2026 — Ideogram 4.5 (Paid Plans):** Ideogram 4.5 image generation now supported in Runway MCP.
- **Oct 1, 2026 — OpenAI Dots x Runway (All Plans):** Runway is now available inside OpenAI's "Dots" product — a Dots agent can plan shots and hand them to Runway to "shoot," with the finished spot ready asynchronously (marketed as "brief your dot before bed... finished spot is waiting in the morning").
- **Sep 29, 2026 — ElevenLabs V4 (Paid Plans):** ElevenLabs' newest, most expressive TTS model now available inside Runway for audio generation.

Just prior to the window (context, not re-logged as new): Sep 23 DaVinci Resolve plugin + Runway MCP on Cursor; Sep 22 Brand Kits in Runway MCP; Sep 18 Kling models (O3 4K, 3.0 Standard, O3 Standard, 3.0 Motion Control) added to Runway MCP; Sep 17 Runway MCP for Grok Bot + Enhance Frame Rate tool; Sep 8 Adobe Premiere/After Effects plugins; Sep 4 new Team Plan.

## Assessment

Runway's strategy continues to be a **model-agnostic aggregator/orchestration layer** (Model Router, MCP marketplace, Agent) rather than competing purely on in-house model quality — this window's additions (Dots integration, Ideogram 4.5, Seedance Draft Mode, ElevenLabs V4) all fit that pattern. The absence of a new Runway-trained video model for ten-plus months (since Gen-4.5, Dec 2025) continues to stand in contrast to the pace of model releases from Lightricks, Kuaishou/Kling, and ByteDance/Seedance.
