# Kling 3.0 / Kling O3 Launch: Omni Architecture with 4K and Native Audio (February 2026)

**Source:** https://www.genaintel.com/guides/kling-3-0-kling-o3-release-features-2026
**Date:** 2026-02-04
**Retrieved:** 2026-05-19

## Content

Kuaishou officially launched Kling 3.0 (also branded as Kling Video O3 / Kling O3) on February 4, 2026. The release was immediately available to all users with no waitlist.

### Architecture
Built on Kuaishou's "Omni One" unified architecture — a single engine handling text-to-video, image-to-video, and video editing.

### Key Features
- **Native 4K output** — upgraded from the previous 1080p maximum; texture detail, edge sharpness, and production quality categorically improved.
- **Native audio generation** — video and audio generate together; lip-sync and facial expressions automatically matched to dialogue.
- **Multi-shot storyboarding** — up to 6 connected shots per single request, up to 15-second clips total.
- **Physics-accurate motion** — models gravity, balance, deformation, collision, and inertia; eliminates common AI video motion artifacts.
- **Multi-modal input** — combines text prompt + reference images (for character appearance), reference video (for motion transfer), and multiple character references simultaneously.
- **60fps** playback via the Omni One engine.
- **Multilingual audio creation** natively.
- **Character consistency** across scenes maintained.

### Benchmark Performance (as of release)
- Kling 3.0 holds the #1 ELO score of 1,243 on AI video benchmarks at launch, ahead of Google Veo 3.1, Runway Gen-4.5, and Pika 2.2.

### Availability
No waitlist; available to all users on Kling AI's platform immediately on launch.

### Kling O3 vs. Kling 3.0 Naming
"Kling O3" is the API/developer-facing name; "Kling 3.0" is the consumer product name. Both refer to the same underlying model.
