# LTX Studio: Video to Video Feature Launch (May 11, 2026)

**Source:** https://ltx.studio/release-notes
**Date:** 2026-05-11
**Retrieved:** 2026-05-25

## Content

LTX Studio launched "Video to Video" on May 11, 2026 as a new feature within the studio platform.

### Key Details

Three control modes are available:
- **Pose** — Lock the structural pose of characters/objects and transform style
- **Depth** — Use depth map to maintain spatial composition while changing aesthetics
- **Edges** — Edge-controlled generation for preserving structural outlines

Users upload a reference video, choose a control mode, and generate with precision. This allows creators to:
- Restyle existing footage completely while preserving structure
- Create stylistic variants of the same scene
- Transform real footage into animated or stylized outputs

This feature was listed under "New Features" and categorized under "LTX Studio" on the official release notes page.
