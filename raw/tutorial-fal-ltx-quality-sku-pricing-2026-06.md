## Content

Third-party blog matrix of fal.ai LTX endpoints. Not checked against fal.ai's pricing page.

- **Fast tier:** 0.04 USD per second at 1080p. 1440p is 2x, 4K is 4x that rate.
- **Pro tier:** 0.06 USD per second at 1080p for image-to-video; 0.08 USD per second for text-to-video.
- **Quality SKU:** billed per megapixel at 0.0024075 USD, or 0.0027075 USD with LoRA fine-tuning.
- **Audio:** all three tiers include native audio. No 720p tier; 1080p is the floor on all endpoints.
- **Replicate (undated comparison):** LTX 2.0 at 0.05 USD/s on Replicate vs 0.04 USD/s on fal.ai. Predates the LTX-2.3 rates above.
- **Replicate video generally (June 2026 source):** roughly 0.07 to 0.25 USD/s depending on model; output-based billing.
- **Relative:** one source puts fal 20 to 40 percent cheaper for models on both platforms. Indicative only.

Verify against fal.ai and replicate.com pricing pages before budgeting.
