LTX relevance: no LTX-specific price change on fal recorded in October. The pattern (two GPU hikes in one week, a 2x image-edit hike) means anyone self-hosting LTX on fal serverless GPUs, or comparing LTX's per-second API price to fal-hosted competitors, should re-check fal rates before budgeting.
