# LTX Studio April 2026 Feature Drop: Canvas Collaborative Workspace & Seedance 2.0 Integration

**Source:** https://ltx.studio/release-notes
**Date:** 2026-04-26
**Retrieved:** 2026-05-19

## Content

LTX Studio launched two significant product updates in late April 2026:

### Canvas (Collaborative Infinite Workspace)

Released in late April 2026, Canvas is a collaborative infinite workspace embedded inside LTX Studio. Key details:

- Teams can generate, iterate, and align on concepts in real time or asynchronously without switching tools.
- Combines image and video moodboards, visual ideation, and team collaboration in one place.
- Users can move seamlessly from concept to production, building storyboards, generating video using multiple models, adding audio, editing on the timeline, and exporting without leaving the platform.
- Designed to serve as the primary visual ideation layer before committing to full production.

### Seedance 2.0 Integration (April 26, 2026)

LTX Studio integrated Seedance 2.0 (ByteDance) into its Gen Space on approximately April 26, 2026, announced via the official LTX Studio X account (tweet IDs 2047728882191860049 and 2047728893860475351).

- Available in Standard and Fast variants via the model picker in Gen Space.
- Seedance 2.0 Standard: prioritizes output quality for hero shots, final renders, or client-facing content; longer generation time.
- Seedance 2.0 Fast: prioritizes speed for rapid iteration when exploring ideas, testing prompts, or building rough cuts.
- Three core strengths: native multi-shot generation, character consistency across shots, high-accuracy motion.
- Generates 1080p video with no resolution restrictions.
- Third-party API for Seedance 2.0 was also made live on fal.ai around this date.

### SDR-to-HDR Feature

Also launched in April 2026, a one-click SDR to HDR conversion that turns any existing video into true High Dynamic Range instantly, available within LTX Studio's editing tools.
