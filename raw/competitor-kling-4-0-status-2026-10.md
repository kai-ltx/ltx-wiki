# Kling 4.0 Availability Status, Sep to Oct 2026 (Conflicting Reports)

**Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kling_AI ; https://www.gradually.ai/en/ai-models/kling-4-0/ ; https://www.atlascloud.ai/blog/guides/kling-3.0-turbo-kling-omni ; https://gaga.art/blog/?p=1168
**Date:** 2026-09-28 (Flash preview) to 2026-10-04 (Gradually.ai research date)
**Retrieved:** 2026-10-19

## Content

- **Early access:** A preview called "Kling 4.0 Flash" went to top-tier annual subscribers on 2026-09-28, per Wikipedia's Kling AI entry.
- **Wikipedia (secondary):** lists 4.0 as the stable release and says the full flagship went global in October 2026. Not confirmed against a Kuaishou or kling.ai primary source.
- **Gradually.ai (research date 2026-10-03/04):** treats Kling 4.0 as announced. Full model scheduled for October 2026. Since 2026-09-28 only the creation experience rolled out to a limited group. Concludes Kling 4.0 is still a preview, not a generally available video or API model.
- **Reported specs (Gradually.ai summary, unverified):** stronger motion, multimodal references, integrated audio. 3 to 30 seconds, up to ten keyframes, 4K, stereo audio with lip sync. No independent benchmark published at the time.
- **Prior releases:** Kling 3.0 Turbo launched 2026-06-17 as a speed and cost-optimized model with audio and improved lip sync, alongside a Kling 3.0 Omni upgrade. Original Kling 3.0 launched 2026-02.
- **Adjacent:** Atlas Cloud (vendor blog) reports Seedance 2.5 live on its platform. Vendor source, commercially motivated.

Status: unresolved. Wikipedia and Gradually.ai disagree on whether the flagship is generally available. Verify at kling.ai announcements or Kuaishou press releases before stating GA.
