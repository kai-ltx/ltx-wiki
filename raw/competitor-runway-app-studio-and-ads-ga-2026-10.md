# Runway App Studio (Oct 26) and Runway Ads GA (Oct 27)

**Source:** https://runway.com/news/company-news/introducing-runway-app-studio ; https://www.scriptbyai.com/runway-ai-timeline/
**Date:** 2026-10-26
**Retrieved:** 2026-11-02

## Content

App Studio (official Runway post, Oct 26, 2026):
- Describe a tool in plain language; App Studio writes, tests and deploys a custom app with its own UI that generates image, video and audio using the account's Brand Kits, Workflows, Assets and References. Iterate in plain language, preview, roll back; code stays visible.
- One-click conversion of an Agent session or Workflow into an app.
- Built-in apps: Character Swap, AutoComp (product swap keeping labels accurate), Product Portfolio, 3D Camera Control, Precise Edit, Runway Pro.
- Early-access customer: KPF (architecture), quoted by Associate Principal Andy Christoforou. Other unnamed partners: film studio fly-through planner and title localisation, background plate matching, game-studio character consistency, ad-agency cultural scenes, soundtrack library, spec-sheet product extraction.
- Sharing: private by default; Team/Enterprise share to teammates/projects/workspace; paid plans can publish public links where visitors sign in and pay with their own credits.
- Enterprise controls: apps follow admin model controls; admins can disable App Studio per workspace and control public links; every Enterprise app link requires Runway sign-in; usage visible per app.
- Pricing: included in all paid plans, no add-on; building, running and hosting draw from existing credits. Free users can run built-in and shared apps only.
- Not documented: custom code, webhooks or external APIs inside apps, source export, custom domains, embedding.

Runway Ads: general availability across paid plans on Oct 27, 2026 (connect ad accounts, generate creative variants, publish approved ads), following its Sep 30 announcement. Per ScriptByAI timeline (secondary, updated Oct 28), citing Runway changelog.

No new first-party Runway video model in-window; Gen-4.5 (Dec 2025) remains flagship. Runway's site also lists a "Video Model Licensing" page; one secondary roundup says Runway began licensing model weights to enterprises in October (unverified).
