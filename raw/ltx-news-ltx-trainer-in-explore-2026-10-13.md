# LTX Trainer Arrives in LTX Explore (No-Code Character/Style LoRA Training)

**Source:** https://ltx.io/release-notes
**Date:** 2026-10-13
**Retrieved:** 2026-10-26

## Content

Official LTX release-notes entry dated October 13, 2026, tagged "API".

- LTX Trainer "now has a new, easier way to get started": train a custom **Character** or **Style** LoRA directly in LTX Explore (app.ltx.io), "with no GPU setup or development work required."
- Workflow:
  - Choose Character or Style LoRA, then upload assets.
  - Review clips flagged by **automatic quality checks** before training.
  - Training runs in the background; user gets an **email** when the LoRA is ready.
  - Test the LoRA inside Explore, or **download** it for use in your own workflow.
- "The open-source LTX Trainer workflow is still available for those who prefer to run it themselves."
- Context: LTX Explore launched 2026-07-20 as the no-code self-service tier; LTX Trainer had a unified-framework upgrade on 2026-06-17 (video/audio/cross-modal/IC-LoRA modes, agentic config generation).
- Not stated in release notes: pricing per training job, step limits, data retention policy for uploaded training assets.
- A separate search summary (cgchannel, early Oct 2026) states content uploaded to the LTX web app may be used to train AI models; check current LTX terms before uploading confidential footage. Not verified against the LTX terms page this run.
